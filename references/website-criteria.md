# Website Criteria
Use only the parts relevant to the question. Evaluate business usefulness, not personal taste.

## Purpose, structure and navigation
Identify the audience, visitor task, offer, evidence and next action. Distinguish service, commerce, information, support, portfolio and membership purposes.
Map page groups and relationships before choosing menus. Use familiar labels, useful groups and relevant footer links. Test mobile open/close, keyboard access, active states, dropdowns and destinations. Use breadcrumbs, search and filters when content depth warrants them; impose no universal item or click limit.
For comparisons normalize the visitor task, audience and offer. A pattern may fit one site and be wrong for another.

## Headlines, heroes and sections
A hero is the prominent opening area, not a mandatory giant image. It should clarify the page through headline, supporting copy, appropriate next action and optional visual.
Service headlines can name service/outcome; product headlines can explain benefit/difference; information pages often need a direct topic/question. Do not force sales heroes onto support articles.
Prefer images that demonstrate the service, product or result, preserve meaningful mobile crops and leave copy readable. Generic decoration should earn its space.
Give each section a job: explain, demonstrate, compare, build trust, address objections, guide action or supply detail. Check sequence, repetition, omissions and visual weight. Place proof near claims.
Plan home, service/product, about, contact/booking, pricing and article/category pages only when needed.

## Content, usability and accessibility foundations
Check accurate approved facts, scanning, reading order, clear headings, spacing, contrast, link labels, focus, touch controls, keyboard operation, zoom and overflow. Test form instructions, errors, recovery and completion where authorized.
Name the actual conditions tested. Basic checks, screenshots and automated results do not certify full accessibility.
Include relevant empty, not-found and failed-media states. Keep essential answers available in text, not only pictures or video.

## Media
Choose media for explanation or trust. Video can demonstrate; a GIF can illustrate a short loop; animation can provide feedback. Check controls, fallback, captions/text alternatives, reduced motion and disruptive autoplay/overlays.
Plan dimensions and loading to avoid material layout movement and delay; measure under recorded conditions when it matters rather than inventing a universal speed target.
Website review owns the asset's role and placement; use video/graphic companions for specialist asset creation or detailed critique.
Verify ownership/licence for fonts and images. Give informative photos meaningful alternatives; decorative images may use empty alternatives. Never present fictional people as real customers.

## Reusable templates
Specify page, section, header and footer patterns, required/optional fields, responsive behavior, variants, style rules, shared/local parts, owner/version and affected-page checks.
Distinguish linked global components from copied starter templates. Repeated appearance alone does not prove shared implementation.
Prove a representative page and shared header/footer before expanding a new build. Preserve local copy when changing shared layouts.
Portable specifications travel between builders; native import files may not. Record actual adapter differences.

## Basic SEO and answer-ready content
Use helpful original answers, clear identity, meaningful titles/headings/addresses, relevant internal links and image alternatives. Avoid thin duplicate keyword pages. Check metadata and intended indexing when accessible.
When URLs change, map old to new, check authorized redirects, internal/inbound destinations and preferred canonical addresses. Large migrations remain separate.
Keep previews out of search appropriately, then recheck indexing for authorized launch. Structured data must match real content and applicable current guidance; AEO is not a universal technical standard.
No rankings, traffic, conversions or answer-engine citations are guaranteed.
